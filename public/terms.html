Terms for using this contact site

This page sets basic expectations for acceptable use of the contact form.

Purpose

This site exists for legitimate contact regarding Continental projects, support, reports, and product feedback.

Acceptable use

Do not use the form for spam, impersonation, harassment, automated flooding, or unlawful content.

Submission handling

Messages are reviewed manually and may be ignored when they are abusive, duplicative, or operationally unsafe to process.

No guarantee of response

Submitting the form does not create a support contract, service-level agreement, or guaranteed reply window.

Security

Do not send passwords, tokens, private keys, card data, or other secrets through this form.
